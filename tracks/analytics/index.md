# Phase 3 · Data analytics
{: .no_toc }

**Time budget:** 6 weeks · **Prereqs:** [Phase 1](../databases/index.md); M8 is helpful but not required

Turning data into decisions someone will act on. The five modules cover the thinking (metrics
that mean something), the rigour (statistics and experiments), the craft (exploratory analysis),
the presentation (visualization and dashboards) and the delivery (a memo that changes a decision).
Each build reuses the Olist data so that by the end there is one coherent analytical story.

| Module | Weeks | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [M12 · Analytical thinking and metrics](12-metrics.md) | 1 | A metrics tree with definitions that survive scrutiny |
| [M13 · Statistics for analysts](13-statistics.md) | 2 | Interpret uncertainty; design and read an A/B test |
| [M14 · Exploratory data analysis](14-eda.md) | 1 | A disciplined EDA that finds the real story |
| [M15 · Visualization and dashboards](15-visualization.md) | 1 | Charts that make one point; a real dashboard |
| [M16 · Communicating analysis](16-communication.md) | 1 | A one-page memo with a recommendation |

**Stack:** DuckDB and the M8 warehouse for SQL, Polars and pandas, Plotly and Matplotlib, Jupyter, Evidence or Metabase for dashboards.

**Backbone reading:** *OpenIntro Statistics*, *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* (chapters 1–5), *Storytelling with Data*.
