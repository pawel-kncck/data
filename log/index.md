# Learning log

One short entry per week. The point is not documentation; it is forcing a weekly moment of
honesty about what worked, what did not, and what changes next week.

Create an entry by copying [the template](TEMPLATE.md) to `log/YYYY-Wnn.md`, for example
`log/2026-W38.md`. Entries appear below automatically.

## Writing a good entry

- Fifteen minutes, no more.
- "What was hard" is the most valuable section. Be specific: *window frames with `RANGE`*, not *SQL*.
- If the week's time budget was missed, say by how much and why. No excuses needed, just the number.
- End with exactly one thing to change next week.
